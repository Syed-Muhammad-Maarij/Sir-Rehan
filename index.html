The approach

How a chapter goes from confusing to conquered.

01

Draw it first

Every topic starts with a diagram: free-body forces, field lines, ray paths, energy flows. If you can draw it, you can solve it.

Why does a falling ball reach terminal velocity? → drag grows until W = D
02

Measure it in a practical

Before the theory sets, you take real-feeling readings: time 20 swings, read the meter, plot the graph and get the gradient the mark scheme wants.

T² = (4π²/g) l → gradient = 4π²/g → g = 9.8 m s⁻²
03

Drill with real papers

Practice comes straight from past papers, topic by topic first, then full timed papers as the exam gets close.

9702/22 · 9702/33 · 5054/22 → timed, marked, reviewed
04

Write like the examiner

Mark schemes and examiner reports show the exact wording that earns marks. You learn to write answers that collect every one.

"resultant force is zero" not "forces cancel" · units on every heading
Physics Practicals

The practical paper, rehearsed until it's easy.

Take readings with real instrument error, fill a results table, plot the graph, draw the gradient triangle and evaluate. Each step is marked the way Cambridge marks it. Free, with no sign-up.

01

Simple pendulum

Time oscillations, plot T² against l and find g from the gradient, with its uncertainty.
02

Hooke's law

Load a spring, read the extension against a metre rule and find the spring constant.
03

Refraction

Trace rays with pins through a glass block, measure i and r, and find the refractive index.
04

I–V characteristics

Vary the p.d., read the ammeter and voltmeter, and compare a resistor with a filament lamp.

Questions

Before you message.

Which syllabuses are taught?

Cambridge O Level Physics (5054), IGCSE Physics (0625) and AS & A Level Physics (9702), including the practical papers: 5054 Papers 3 and 4, 0625 Papers 5 and 6, and 9702 Papers 3 and 5.

How do I join a class?

Press Join a class to create your account and choose your course. You can also fill in the form below or send a WhatsApp message with your level and syllabus. Current batch timings, venues and fees are shared there.

Are the Physics Practicals free?

Yes. The practicals, the physics tools and the Paper Vault are all free to use, with no sign-up. Everything runs in your browser.

Can the online practicals replace real lab work?

No, but they make it far easier. The marks in a practical paper come from how you record, plot and analyse readings, and that is exactly what you drill here. In the real lab you then know what to measure and how to write it down.

What's the difference between a mark scheme and an examiner report?

A mark scheme lists the answers and where each mark is given. An examiner report explains how candidates actually performed and the mistakes that cost the most marks. Read both after every paper.
